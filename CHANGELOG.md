# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-11-09
- On a tile two rows high, a monitor's row under the sources: its name and state on top, a key for the picture and
  one for the power. Choose a picture switch and a power switch in the inspector.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-11-09
- The logos are built in and take the screen's colours: no more dark square behind them, and nothing is fetched from
  the internet any more.
- Logos show on every screen, the ones without pictures too.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-11-09
- First version: a row of keys with logos for a select, the chosen one in the accent colour; a tap chooses another or
  runs a script with it.
